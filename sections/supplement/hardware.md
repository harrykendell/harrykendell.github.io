> [!INFO] Use marine-appropriate stainless (and avoid galvanic surprises)
>
> - **A2** stainless is roughly **304** grade; **A4** is roughly **316** grade (better chloride resistance).
> - Prefer **A4/316** for any hardware that regularly gets wet (especially salt/brackish).
> - Where stainless mates to aluminium (riggers, backstays), use isolating washers and a corrosion inhibitor to reduce galvanic corrosion.
> - If the fleet sees brackish water, budget for replacement hardware rather than trying to “save” corroded fasteners.

> [!WARNING] Split washers
> Split/spring washers can crush paint, destroy laminate, and create stress concentrations. **All of which are terrible for boats**.
>
> - Never put a split washer directly on a boat surface
> - Use flat washers to spread the load and protect
> - Bolts with **integral flanges** can substitute for the bolt-head washer but its better to be safe than sorry if unsure
> 
> **Typical stack:**
> `Bolt head → Flat washer → Hull/Rigger → Flat washer → (Split/spring washer if used) → Nut`the boat
