# Quick Reference Checklists

## After every outing (Beginner)

- Rinse boat (especially after saltwater) and wash inside/out with mild soap.
- Wipe dry (microfibre) and open hatches briefly to ventilate; close after dry.
- Wipe seat tracks and seat wheels; remove grit.
- Visual check: fin/rudder, hull, riggers, footplate.
- Gate check: top nuts tight; gates close fully.
- Log/report any impact damage.

## Weekly / maintenance day (Intermediate)

- Remove seats; clean wheels and tracks; inspect for flat spots.
- Disassemble and clean gates (oarlocks); check bushings/washers.
- Inspect and retighten critical fasteners (rigger bolts, pin fasteners, stretcher fasteners).
- Inspect steering cables, toe hinge bolt/nut, rudder yoke.
- Check for cracks in seats, riggers, footplates.

## Monthly (Intermediate)

- Audit spares and consumables; restock regatta box.
- Inspect wiring connectors for corrosion; clean and re-seal as needed.
- Inspect hull for developing soft spots/delamination (tap test on suspect areas).

## Annual / end-of-season (Intermediate/Professional)

- Replace seat wheels as a set (where applicable).
- Full rigging audit (pins, top nuts, washer stacks, footplates).
- Machine polish/wax (if part of the fleet finish policy).
- Service steering cables; replace if frayed.
- Review damage logs; schedule professional repairs.
