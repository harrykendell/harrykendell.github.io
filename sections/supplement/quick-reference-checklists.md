# Quick reference checklists

<!-- Content

 -->

These checklists are designed for fast use on the pontoon or on maintenance day. Detailed procedures live in the maintenance/repairs sections.

> [!INFO] Recording
> If you find damage or a stop‑use condition, take a photo, tag the equipment, and log it. “Someone else will deal with it” is how repairs spiral.

## Pre‑outing checklist (boat)

- Hull/deck: no obvious cracks, deep scrapes, exposed fibres, or soft spots
- Buoyancy: hatch covers seated, seals intact, bungs fitted; no water sloshing
- Riggers/pins: no movement at hull; top nuts/retainers present and tight
- Gates latch fully; no abnormal wobble
- Seat runs smoothly end‑to‑end (no grinding)
- Footplate clamps tight; heel restraints present and intact
- Steering (if fitted): centres and moves smoothly through full travel
- Fin/rudder secure; no wobble or binding

## Post‑outing checklist (boat)

- Rinse hull, riggers, tracks, footplate area (remove grit/algae)
- Wipe tracks and wheels; seat still runs smoothly
- Dry cockpit and ventilate briefly; close hatches after dry
- Report new damage immediately (photos + location)

## Monthly maintenance checklist (boat)

- Deep clean: tracks, wheels, riggers, pins, footplate hardware
- Rigger inspection: no movement, no cracks/bends, stays intact (if fitted)
- Gate service where gritty/noisy: clean/rebuild, restore washer stacks
- Footplate scan: clamps, shoe mounts, heel restraint anchors secure
- Steering scan: cable/rope condition, routing, no chafe points
- Hardware audit: missing washers, corroded fasteners, nylocs worn
- Damage scan: tap test around known damage; check fin box/rudder area

## Oars checklist

- Shafts straight; no cracks or soft spots
- Blade‑to‑shaft joints intact; no movement
- Collars secure; inboard consistent across the set
- Sleeves intact; no cracks; not rotating
- Grips intact; no sharp edges; suede not lifting
- After outing: rinse, wipe grips, rack securely

## After impact checklist (boat)

After any collision/grounding/fin strike/bridge strike:

- Stop and inspect immediately; do not “row it home” if uncertain
- Photograph damage with scale; mark boundary with tape
- Tap test around area for delamination
- Check fin box/rudder and buoyancy compartments for leaks
- Tag out if any soft spot, crack from load points, or suspected core ingress
- Log the incident and damage
