# Start here

This site is split into **Maintenance** (routine care, troubleshooting, and replacement) and **Repairs** (structural/adhesive work and rebuilds).

## Capability tiers

- **Beginner:** basic club members; reversible tasks; cleaning; simple checks.
- **Intermediate:** trained members; replacements; adjustments; controlled disassembly/reassembly.
- **Advanced:** equipment officers/specialists only; structural repairs; adhesive/composite work; harness builds.

If a procedure includes a **Stop-use** or **Escalation** warning, treat it as mandatory.
