# Guides

## Before outing (boat)

- **Hull/deck**
  - Visible cracks, deep scrapes, soft spots, exposed fibres
  - Any sharp edges or protrusions
- **Buoyancy**
  - Hatch covers present, seated correctly, seals intact
  - Vent/condensation bungs fitted correctly (as appropriate for the boat)
- **Steering & fin**
  - Rudder moves freely and returns; no binding
  - Cables/lines intact and tensioned; pulleys free
  - Fin/skeg secure (no wobble)
- **Riggers & hardware**
  - Rigger bolts tight; no movement at feet
  - No visible cracks (esp. around welds and stress points)
  - Gates/oarlocks close fully and lock; no excessive play
- **Running gear**
  - Seat runs smoothly; no wheel binding
  - Tracks/slides clean enough to run smoothly
- **Footstretcher**
  - Shoes secure; straps intact
  - Heel restraints present and functional
- **Required equipment (where applicable)**
  - Lights present and working (if required)
  - Bow ball present and secure

### 1.2 Before outing (oars)

- Buttons/collars secure and symmetrical
- Sleeves not cracked; collar/sleeve alignment correct
- Oarlocks fit and rotate smoothly
- Blades: no edge splits, major chips, or delamination

### 1.3 After outing (routine clean + dry)

- Rinse hull and cockpit (fresh water)
- Wipe down hull, cockpit, riggers, gates, footstretcher
- Clean tracks/slides (remove grit), wipe seat wheels if dirty
- Drain any water; check compartments if water ingress suspected
- Open hatches / vents as per storage standard to ventilate and dry
- Report any new issues and tag/log immediately

### 1.4 Weekly fleet pass (maintainers)

- Fast scan of all boats:
  - Rigger bolt tightness, gate function, obvious hull damage
  - Steering function on boats with steering
  - Track/runner wear indicators
  - Hatch covers/seals present and in good condition
- Remove grit build-up from commonly used boats (tracks, seat wheels)
- Update parts needs list (washers/bolts/bungs/covers)

### 1.5 Monthly / seasonal (maintainers)

- Deep clean rotation (see §2.3)
- Corrosion/fastener management rotation
- Wear parts inspection:
  - Tracks/runners, seat wheels/axles, gates/oarlocks, footstraps/heel restraints
- Trailer check (tyres, straps, lights, hitch, winch if fitted)
- Rack/trestle condition check

## Routine Care (Standard Procedures)

### 2.1 Post-outing clean (standard)

**Tools/consumables:**

- Bucket, mild soap, sponge/soft brush
- Microfibre towels
- Fresh water hose (gentle flow)
- Track/seat wheel wipe cloth

**Steps:**

1. Rinse hull and cockpit.
2. Light soap wash for hull and cockpit contact areas.
3. Rinse again.
4. Wipe dry (hull, cockpit, riggers, gates).
5. Clean tracks/slides and wipe seat wheels if needed.
6. Drain/check compartments if any water is suspected.
7. Open hatches/vents for ventilation (as per storage standard).
8. Rack correctly.

### 2.2 Drying & ventilation standard

- After washing, ensure compartments can ventilate:
  - Open hatches and/or vent bungs as defined for each boat
- Do not leave boats sealed wet.
- If water ingress is suspected:
  - Inspect compartments, drain, log, and tag as needed.

### 2.3 Deep clean (scheduled)

**Tools/consumables:**

- Hull cleaner (approved)
- Vinegar (for mild deposits)
- Microfibres, soft brushes, small parts tray
- Labels or tape for parts

**Steps:**

1. Photograph current rigging setup if you will remove parts.
2. Remove: seats, gates/oarlocks (if appropriate), loose accessories.
3. Wash hull thoroughly; address sculling marks and grime.
4. Clean cockpit: footstretcher, heel cups, under-seat areas.
5. Clean tracks/slides and seat wheels; remove embedded grit.
6. Inspect for:
   - Missing parts (washers, bolts, bungs, hatch covers)
   - Loose hardware and developing cracks
   - Wear indicators on tracks/wheels/gates
7. Reassemble; verify washer stacks and bolt engagement.
8. Update log with findings and parts needed.

### 2.4 Polish / wax cycle (as scheduled)

- Wash and dry fully first.
- Apply polish/wax (approved) to hull only (avoid grip surfaces).
- Buff to finish.
- Record date in maintenance log.

## Storage & Transport

### 3.1 Racking standard

- Use correct support points; avoid point loads.
- Keep hulls clear of sharp edges and protrusions.
- Ensure adequate clearance for riggers and fins.
- Store boats in consistent orientation (define per rack: hull-up / hull-down).
- Keep hatches/vents in the defined storage state (see §2.2).

### 3.2 Carrying & handling (standard)

- Lift with enough people; coordinate “up / walk / down”.
- Avoid dragging hulls; avoid knocking riggers/gates on racks/doors.

### 3.3 Trailer loading & transport

- Load order and support spacing per trailer standard.
- Use proper straps and protection where straps contact hulls/riggers.
- Secure seats and loose parts (strap or remove).
- Pre-departure: tyres, hitch, lights, straps, load movement check.

### 3.4 Roof topping

- Only approved racks and straps.
- Protect contact points; ensure no strap rub on carbon edges.
- Check after a short drive; then periodically on longer trips.

## Components: Inspection, Adjustment, Replacement

### 4.1 Hull, deck, buoyancy compartments

- Hatch covers: fit, seals, cords, gaskets
- Vent/condensation bungs: present and correct for storage/rowing
- Water in compartment: diagnose source; dry and log
- Paint/gelcoat chips: record and treat to prevent water ingress

### 4.2 Steering system

- Rudder alignment and neutral position
- Cable/line integrity and tension
- Pulleys, clamps, yoke condition
- Common faults: seized steering, slipping clamps, frayed lines

### 4.3 Fin / skeg system

- Fin security check (no movement)
- Fin box condition (cracks, separation, leaks)
- Replacement procedure lives in Repairs (§5)

### 4.4 Riggers & hardware

- Crack inspection points (welds, bends, high-stress regions)
- Bolt tightness and correct washer stacks
- Gate/oarlock function and bushing play
- Corrosion management:
  - periodic bolt removal/cleaning where needed
  - replace corroded fasteners promptly

#### Washer stack reference

- Maintain a consistent washer stack standard for each boat/rigger type.
- Keep a “known good” reference photo or diagram per boat class.

### 4.5 Running gear: tracks/runners, seats, wheels

- Tracks/runners: inspect wear, grooves, missing anodizing, looseness
- Seat: wheel condition, axle play, running smoothness
- Replace worn wheels/axles or tracks as needed (see §5)

### 4.6 Footstretcher, shoes, heel restraints

- Shoe security and wear
- Strap integrity
- Heel restraints: present and functioning; replace if stretched/broken
- Footplate bolts and adjusters: no slop, no stripped threads

### 4.7 Oars, sleeves, collars, buttons, grips

- Sleeve cracks and collar/button security
- Pitch system condition (bushings, inserts)
- Handle/grip condition (suede/plastic): cleaning and replacement schedule
- Blade edges: chips, cracks, delamination (see §5.9)

### 4.8 Electronics (if fitted)

- Wiring in boat (routing, connectors, strain relief)
- Speakers not working (diagnosis + replacement)
- Lights (if fitted/required): function check and battery management

## Repairs (When it’s gone wrong)

### 5.1 Damage triage (standard)

- Identify category:
  - **Structural / watertight / steering-critical** → tag `OUT OF SERVICE`
  - **Functional wear** → tag `LIMITED USE` or `MONITOR`
  - **Cosmetic** → log and schedule
- Photograph and record before starting work.
- If unsure: escalate to maintainer/boatman.

### 5.2 Cosmetic: paint chips, scratches, scuffs

- Clean, dry, and assess depth.
- For superficial scuffs: polish cycle.
- For chips to laminate/gelcoat: seal and finish per club procedure.

### 5.3 Small puncture

- Locate full extent; check for water ingress.
- Repair using approved patch method (Composite Repairs §6).

### 5.4 Crush / core damage

- Tag out of service.
- Assess area, core condition, and skin integrity.
- Repair via core replacement/patch as required (Composite Repairs §6).

### 5.5 Loose insert / enlarged hole / stripped threads

- Tag depending on location (rigger bolts usually out of service).
- Repair insert, re-bond, or drill/fill/re-drill as appropriate.
- Standardise washer stack after reassembly.

### 5.6 Broken fin / fin replacement

- Remove old fin, clean fin box, refit and seal as specified for that boat.
- Verify alignment and watertightness.

### 5.7 Hatch cover replacement (cover, cord, seal)

- Replace cord and/or cover.
- Inspect seal/gasket; replace if compressed or torn.
- Leak test where practical.

### 5.8 Steering faults

- **Steering seized:** identify binding point; clean/replace pulleys/lines.
- **Rudder cable/line replacement:** replace as a matched pair where needed.
- **Rudder alignment:** neutral + straight-line tracking check.

### 5.9 Footplate / shoes / heel restraints

- Replace worn/broken shoes and straps.
- Replace heel restraints immediately if failed.
- Verify bolt tightness and adjuster integrity.

### 5.10 Running gear replacement

- Replace runners/tracks when worn or loose.
- Replace seat wheels/axles when flat-spotted or binding.
- Verify smooth travel and correct seating.

### 5.11 Oar repairs

- Replace oar handle/suede/grips
- Button/collar replacement and re-setting gearing symmetry
- Sleeve replacement; pitch bushing maintenance
- Spoon/blade delamination repair (often professional if extensive)

## Composite Repairs (Structural methods)

> Only trained maintainers/boatman.

### 6.1 Patches (skin repair)

- Define repair area, taper, patch schedule, cure, fair, finish.

### 6.2 Reinforcement (bow/saxboard/deck edges)

- Reinforce recurring impact areas.
- Document reinforcement layers and finish.

### 6.3 Splash guard

- Repair or replace mounts and seals.
- Ensure no sharp edges or snag points.

## Rigging Standards

### 7.1 Tools

- Tape measure, straight edge
- Pitch meter or bushing-based pitch method
- Height stick (oarlock height)
- Levels / plumb line
- Log sheet / rigging table

### 7.2 Definitions (glossary)

- Span/spread
- Gate pitch
- Gate height
- Foot stretcher position/height
- Inboard/outboard
- Blade gearing

### 7.3 Measurement workflow (standard)

1. Confirm boat is level and supported.
2. Measure and set:
   - Span/spread
   - Gate height
   - Pitch
3. Set footstretcher position and height.
4. Verify symmetry (port/starboard).
5. Record settings to rigging log/table.
6. Apply change control:
   - Only approved people update “club default” rigs.
   - Temporary changes must be reset and logged.
